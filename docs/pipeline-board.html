Escrow clock

 

Each open escrow on its own timeline — Day 0 is the contract’s acceptance date and the finish is its real close of escrow, so a 21-day escrow and a 45-day escrow each scale to their own length. Until an acceptance date is on the record the day count falls back to time-in-stage and is marked provisional.

Contingency calculator

Day 0 = acceptance

The CR math from the guide: count every day from acceptance — the RPA or the last counter signature — and roll any date landing on a weekend or federal holiday forward to the next business day. Investigation 7 days, loan and appraisal 14. Set the real days-to-COE from the contract; the walk-through and close scale off it.

RPA page 1, 3B carries the COE — not every escrow is 30 days, so read it off the contract and check the counters.
